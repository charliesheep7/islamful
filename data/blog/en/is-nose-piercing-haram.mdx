**There is no single ruling to attribute to every scholar. For women, the cited Hanafi guidance permits nose piercing; other guidance is more restrictive. For men, the cited Hanafi answer does not permit it.** The relevant questions are whose scholarly guidance you follow, the type of piercing, and how the jewelry is displayed.

## What do the cited scholars say?

| Question                          | Published guidance                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nose piercing for women           | [Faraz A. Khan’s Hanafi answer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/are-nose-rings-allowed-for-women/) permits it as a recognized exception for adornment.                                                                |
| Does custom matter?               | [Faraz Rabbani](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/ear-nose-piercings-for-women/) specifies customary acceptance in one’s area or among upright Muslims.                                                                 |
| Is this the same in every school? | [Shazia Ahmad’s Shafi’i-context answer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/if-i-get-my-nose-pierced-do-i-need-to-conceal-it-from-men/) describes a difference from Hanafi guidance and separately discusses concealment. |
| Nose piercing for men             | The cited [Hanafi answer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/are-nose-rings-allowed-for-women/) does not extend the exception to men.                                                                                    |

This is a comparison of identifiable answers, not a claim that all four schools agree. It also does not establish a ruling for every septum piercing, placement or other body modification.

## Is permission to pierce the same as permission to display it?

No. The decision to get a piercing and the rules for showing adornment to non-mahram men are separate questions. The [answer about concealing a nose piercing](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/if-i-get-my-nose-pierced-do-i-need-to-conceal-it-from-men/) discusses that distinction. A short “halal” label is therefore incomplete if your actual concern is wearing the jewelry in public.

## What to ask a scholar about your situation

Explain whether you already have the piercing or are considering one, which placement you mean, the scholarly guidance you follow and the setting in which the jewelry would be worn. If you converted to Islam with an existing piercing, describe that situation directly instead of assuming an answer about a new piercing settles every practical detail.

A useful question is: “Under the guidance I follow, is this particular piercing permitted, and are there separate rules about displaying it?” That gives the scholar the context missing from a generic yes-or-no search.

Do not infer unrelated rules about rings, clothing or other piercings from this comparison. [Explore the topic guide](/haram-check) for related questions, and use the linked original answers to check the reasoning. The guide is general information, not a personal fatwa.
