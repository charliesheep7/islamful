Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam: a pilgrimage to Makkah that is obligatory once in a lifetime for every Muslim who is able. Allah says that pilgrimage to the House is a duty owed to Him by those who are able to make the journey ([Surah Aal 'Imran, 3:97](https://quran.com/3/97)). Done sincerely, it is a chance to start again. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that whoever performs Hajj for Allah's pleasure, without sexual relations or sin, returns as if newly born ([Bukhari 1521](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1521)).

This guide gives the sequence of the rites in order. It is a religious overview, not a travel manual. Permits, registration, health requirements and group arrangements are set each year by the Saudi authorities and your Hajj operator, so check their current official instructions. Scholars also differ on some details, such as what is required to make up for a mistake, so take your own questions to a qualified scholar or your group's guide.

## The three ways to perform Hajj

- **Tamattu':** perform Umrah first, leave ihram, then enter ihram again for Hajj on the 8th of Dhul-Hijjah. A sacrifice is required. Most pilgrims travelling from abroad do this.
- **Qiran:** combine Umrah and Hajj in one continuous ihram. A sacrifice is also required.
- **Ifrad:** perform Hajj alone, without Umrah. No sacrifice is required.

The Quran addresses this directly: complete Hajj and Umrah for Allah, and whoever combines Umrah with Hajj should offer what sacrifice is easy for them ([Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:196](https://quran.com/2/196)). If you are doing Tamattu', read our [Umrah guide](/blog/how-to-perform-umrah-step-by-step) first, since you will complete it before the Hajj days begin.

## Before ihram

Hajj takes place in the well-known months, and the Quran adds that there is to be no obscenity, wickedness or quarrelling in Hajj ([Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:197](https://quran.com/2/197)). Before you set out, repay debts where you can, seek forgiveness from people you have wronged, and make sure your funds are lawful. Learn the sequence below in advance, because the crowds leave little time to work it out on the spot.

Ihram is the state you enter at the miqat with the intention for your rite. Men wear two unstitched white cloths. Women wear ordinary modest clothing. A full wash (see our [ghusl guide](/blog/how-to-perform-ghusl)) and two rak'ahs of prayer are recommended beforehand. In ihram, cutting hair or nails, using perfume, hunting and marital relations are forbidden, and men do not wear stitched clothing or cover the head. Recite the Talbiyah: Labbayk Allahumma labbayk, labbayk la sharika laka labbayk, innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.

## The rites in order

**8th of Dhul-Hijjah, Mina.** Enter ihram for Hajj if you are doing Tamattu', and go to Mina. Pray Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and the next Fajr there, shortening the four rak'ah prayers, and spend the night in worship.

**9th, Arafah.** After sunrise go to the plain of Arafah. This is the central rite. The Prophet announced "Hajj is Arafah," and said that whoever reaches Muzdalifah in the night before Fajr has attended the Hajj ([Tirmidhi 889](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:889)). Spend the afternoon in du'a, dhikr and repentance, and stay until after sunset. If you miss the standing at Arafah, your Hajj for that year is not valid. Use our [dua collection](/dua) to prepare supplications.

**Night of the 9th, Muzdalifah.** After sunset, travel to Muzdalifah, pray Maghrib and Isha together, and rest. Gather pebbles here or later. Leave for Mina after Fajr.

**10th, Eid day.** Four rites follow, normally in this order: stone Jamrat al-Aqabah with seven pebbles; offer the sacrifice, which most pilgrims arrange through an authorised service; shave or trim the hair (men usually shave, women trim a fingertip's length); and perform Tawaf al-Ifadah with Sa'i if it applies to your type of Hajj. After the stoning and the hair, most ihram restrictions lift.

**11th, 12th and 13th, Mina.** Stone all three pillars each afternoon, beginning with the smallest, then the middle, then Jamrat al-Aqabah, with seven pebbles each. The Prophet's announcement says the days of Mina are three, and whoever leaves after two days or stays on has no sin ([Tirmidhi 889](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:889)).

**Farewell.** Before leaving Makkah, perform Tawaf al-Wada' as your last act. Ibn Abbas reported that people were ordered to make the farewell Tawaf their last act before leaving, with an exemption for women in menstruation ([Bukhari 1755](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1755)).

## Common mistakes

- Leaving Arafah early or treating the day as a chance to rest. Use it for du'a.
- Assuming the arrival Tawaf replaces Tawaf al-Ifadah. They are separate.
- Using scented products or arguing while in ihram.
- Panicking over the order of the rites on the 10th. If something is out of order, ask a scholar or your group leader what applies in your case.

## The reward

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that the reward of an accepted Hajj is nothing less than Paradise ([Bukhari 1773](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1773)). The Quran also records the call to the pilgrimage that people have answered from every distant road ([Surah Al-Hajj, 22:27](https://quran.com/22/27)).
