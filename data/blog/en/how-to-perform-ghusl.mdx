![How to Perform Ghusl: Step-by-Step Guide](/static/images/blog/how-to-perform-ghusl.webp)

To do ghusl, wash your whole body with water, making sure you also rinse your mouth and put water into your nose. That is the minimum that makes ghusl valid in the Hanafi school. The full sunnah method adds a few steps before it: say Bismillah, wash your hands and private parts, make wudu, then pour water over your head and the rest of your body.

Ghusl is the full wash that lifts major impurity (janabah). The Quran says, "if you are in a state of full impurity, then take a full bath" ([Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6](https://quran.com/5/6)), and forbids approaching prayer in that state "until you have bathed" ([Surah An-Nisa 4:43](https://quran.com/4/43)).

## The fard of ghusl

Darul Ifta Birmingham, answering according to the Hanafi school, lists three compulsory actions of ghusl ([Darul Ifta Birmingham, fatwa 03298](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/home/?p=13339)):

1. Rinse the mouth with water.
2. Put water into the nose and blow it out.
3. Wash the whole body.

The same fatwa says that if any of the three is missing, the obligation of ghusl remains. In practice that means no dry patches: check the scalp, behind the ears, the navel, the armpits and between the toes.

This is the Hanafi list. If you follow another school, check its list of obligatory acts with your local imam, since schools count them differently.

## The sunnah of ghusl

The same fatwa lists nine sunnah actions that complete the ghusl and bring extra reward:

- Begin with Bismillah
- Make the intention of purification
- Wash both hands up to the wrists
- Wash off any impurity first
- Make wudu before the ghusl
- Pour water over the entire body three times
- Pour water over the head, then the right shoulder, then the left
- Scrub the body
- Wash each part before the previous one dries

## Step by step: how the Prophet did ghusl

Aishah described the Prophet's ghusl after intercourse in [Sahih Muslim 316a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:316a). Following her description:

1. **Say Bismillah and intend** to purify yourself from major impurity.
2. **Wash your hands.**
3. **Wash your private parts,** pouring water with the right hand onto the left.
4. **Make wudu as you would for prayer,** including rinsing the mouth and nose.
5. **Work wet fingers through the roots of your hair** until the scalp is moistened.
6. **Pour three handfuls of water over your head.**
7. **Pour water over the rest of your body,** right side then left.
8. **Wash your feet** at the end.

As a worked example: you wake up in a state of janabah 20 minutes before Fajr ends. Steps 1 to 8 take about five minutes in a shower, so you can finish ghusl and still pray on time. Check today's exact times with our [prayer times tool](/prayer-times).

## Ghusl dua: what to say

Aishah's description in Sahih Muslim 316a does not mention a special dua for ghusl, and none is needed for it to be valid. What you can say:

- **At the start:** "Bismillah" (In the name of Allah). Darul Ifta Birmingham lists beginning with Bismillah as a sunnah of ghusl.
- **After finishing:** because the sunnah ghusl includes wudu, you can say the testimony the Prophet taught after wudu: "I testify that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the servant of Allah and His Messenger." [Sahih Muslim 234a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:234a) says whoever completes wudu well and says this will have the eight gates of Paradise opened for them. Applying it to ghusl is this guide's suggestion, based on the wudu within ghusl.

The intention is made in the heart. You do not need to say it aloud.

## How to do ghusl without showering

You do not need a shower. When Umm Salama asked about ghusl, the Prophet told her it was enough to throw three handfuls of water on her head and then pour water over herself ([Sahih Muslim 330a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:330a)). Pouring water is the method itself.

With a bucket and jug:

1. Fill a bucket with clean water and stand or sit somewhere the water can drain away.
2. Rinse your mouth and put water in your nose from a cupped hand.
3. Make wudu with the jug.
4. Pour three jugfuls over your head, working water into the roots of your hair.
5. Pour water over your right side, then your left, then any part still dry.

In a bath, sitting in the water can wash the body, but you still need to rinse your mouth and nose separately for the Hanafi fard to be met.

If you cannot use water because you are ill or have none, the same verses allow purifying with clean earth instead ([Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6](https://quran.com/5/6)). See [how to perform tayammum](/blog/how-to-perform-tayammum).

## Common mistakes

- **Forgetting the mouth and nose.** In the Hanafi school these are fard, so a shower without them is not a complete ghusl.
- **Leaving dry spots,** especially the scalp, navel and behind the ears.
- **Undoing braids unnecessarily.** For ghusl after intercourse, Umm Salama was told she did not need to undo closely plaited hair ([Sahih Muslim 330a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:330a)).
- **Repeating out of doubt.** If you washed everything with intention, your ghusl is done.

Once you have finished, you are ready to pray. For the smaller purification, see [how to perform wudu](/blog/how-to-perform-wudu), and if you are new to prayer, start with [how to pray salah for beginners](/blog/how-to-pray-salah-for-beginners).
