To calculate annual Zakat on eligible wealth, establish your Zakat date, value the assets that count, apply the deductions permitted by your chosen guidance, and compare the balance with the nisab. When the conditions apply, the usual annual calculation for these assets is **net zakatable wealth ÷ 40**.

Use the [free Zakat calculator](/zakat-calculator) to work through the numbers. It supports cash, savings, eligible investments, gold and silver valuations, metal purity and a downloadable calculation summary. This guide covers annual wealth Zakat, not Zakat al-Fitr, agricultural produce or livestock.

## 1. Establish your Zakat date

Use a lunar-year basis rather than silently substituting a calendar year. Your anniversary and the assets included on it need to follow the guidance appropriate to your circumstances.

It is misleading to say every newly saved pound must independently sit untouched for a year. [National Zakat Foundation’s lunar-year explanation](https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/is-zakat-binding-only-after-the-passing-of-a-lunar-year/) uses the same anniversary for qualifying cash, metals and business stock acquired during that year. Ask about changes in ownership or a fall below nisab; this guide does not present one school’s treatment of interruptions as universal.

The calculator asks whether your lunar year has completed. “No” and “I am not sure” produce clearly labelled planning results.

## 2. Choose a dated nisab

Nisab is the threshold used to assess qualifying wealth. Gold and silver can produce very different currency thresholds, and authorities use different gram equivalents. [Islamic Relief’s explanation](https://islamic-relief.org/zakat-faq/) discusses the choice of metal.

You can enter a trusted local nisab directly, or calculate one using your own price per gram. The calculator offers these documented conventions:

- [Islamic Relief UK](https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/giving/islamic-giving/zakat/zakat-calculator/): 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver.
- [IslamQA’s pure-metal explanation](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/193553): 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver.

Follow your chosen authority. A price copied from an old article is not today’s valuation. Use the same currency and valuation date throughout; changing the calculator’s currency clears its amounts rather than pretending to convert them.

## 3. List the eligible assets once

Gather account balances, eligible metal valuations, trade assets and other amounts that belong in your calculation. Keep a worksheet showing what each amount represents. Avoid entering the same bank balance under both cash and savings, or counting the same jewellery by both value and weight.

For gold, purity affects the metal value. As an arithmetic illustration, 100 grams at 18 karat represents 75 grams of pure-gold equivalent. Multiply by the appropriate pure-metal price. Do not mistake the weight of stones or other components for gold. For mixed purities, obtain or calculate a combined eligible valuation.

Personal-use jewellery is a genuine area of disagreement. [Islamic Relief’s jewellery guide](https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/giving/islamic-giving/zakat/zakat-on-jewellery/) distinguishes the Hanafi position from conditional exemptions in other schools. Establish which items are included before doing the arithmetic.

Investments also need more than a blanket portfolio percentage. [National Zakat Foundation’s shares guidance](https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/zakat-on-stocks-and-shares/) distinguishes resale holdings from long-term investments and their underlying zakatable assets. Enter the eligible portion you have established. Complex pensions, companies and property interests may need specialist assessment.

## 4. Review deductions

A large loan balance does not automatically mean a zero Zakat calculation. Establish which liabilities your method permits you to deduct.

[National Zakat Foundation’s debt guidance](https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/payment-of-zakat-deductible-liabilities/) distinguishes outstanding liabilities, eligible instalments and future expenses, with conditions on the allowance. It does not support blindly deducting every future bill or an entire long-term loan. Enter only the deduction applicable to your circumstances.

## 5. Check the threshold and calculate

Here is an illustrative calculation in a single currency. **These figures are examples, not current prices or nisab values.**

| Item                                 | Amount |
| ------------------------------------ | -----: |
| Cash and savings                     |  8,000 |
| Eligible metal value                 |  3,000 |
| Other eligible assets                |  1,000 |
| Total assets                         | 12,000 |
| Permitted deductions                 |  2,000 |
| Net zakatable wealth                 | 10,000 |
| Selected nisab                       |  5,000 |
| Annual estimate, with conditions met |    250 |

The net balance reaches nisab. Divide the **whole 10,000** by 40 to get 250; do not divide only the 5,000 excess. The calculator compares amounts in cents, rounds metal valuations to the nearest cent and rounds a fractional cent of the calculated payment upward. It shows these assumptions with the result.
