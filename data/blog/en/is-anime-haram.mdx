**Is watching anime haram in Islam?** There is no single ruling for every series. [Shaykh Irshaad Sedick says that watching animated television is permissible when its content is lawful](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/is-it-permissible-for-me-to-watch-anime/). [Shaykh Abdul-Rahim Reasat notes that contemporary scholars differ about watching videos in general](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/is-it-permissible-to-watch-anime-or-cartoons-in-islam/). Both advise choosing content that does not promote prohibited conduct.

> **Quick answer:** Ask what a particular show contains and promotes, and whether watching it leads you to neglect your obligations. Do not treat every animated series as automatically halal or haram. If you need a ruling on a specific series or scene, consult a qualified scholar.

## The Evidence from Quran and Hadith

Islam does not prohibit entertainment outright. The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) recognized the need for recreation and rest. What Islam does regulate is the **content** of what we consume — whether through our eyes, ears, or hearts.

The Quran reminds believers that hearing and sight are accountable:

> وَلَا تَقْفُ مَا لَيْسَ لَكَ بِهِ عِلْمٌ ۚ إِنَّ السَّمْعَ وَالْبَصَرَ وَالْفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُولَـٰئِكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْئُولًا

> _Wa la taqfu ma laysa laka bihi 'ilm. Inna al-sam'a wal-basara wal-fu'ada kullu ula'ika kana 'anhu mas'ula_

> "And do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge. Indeed, the hearing, the sight, and the heart — about all of those one will be questioned."
> — Surah Al-Isra, 17:36

This verse speaks about accountability for hearing and sight. It does not give an anime-specific ruling; scholars apply broader principles when discussing particular content.

Regarding looking at what is forbidden, the Prophet ﷺ said:

> كُتِبَ عَلَى ابْنِ آدَمَ نَصِيبُهُ مِنَ الزِّنَا مُدْرِكٌ ذَلِكَ لَا مَحَالَةَ فَالْعَيْنَانِ زِنَاهُمَا النَّظَرُ

> _Kutiba 'ala ibni Adama naseebuhu min al-zina mudrikun dhalika la mahalah, fal-'aynaani zinahuma al-nadhar_

> "The son of Adam has been decreed his share of zina, which he will inevitably commit. The zina of the eyes is looking (at what is forbidden)."
> — Narrated by al-Bukhari, 6243; Muslim, 2657

This hadith is one reason to take sexually explicit material seriously. How rules about looking apply to drawn characters is a separate juristic question: [Shaykh Irshaad Sedick distinguishes cartoon images from real people and discusses the role of desire](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/is-it-permissible-for-me-to-watch-anime/).

On the topic of wasting time with amusement that distracts from Allah, the Quran states:

> وَمِنَ النَّاسِ مَن يَشْتَرِي لَهْوَ الْحَدِيثِ لِيُضِلَّ عَن سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ

> _Wa mina al-nasi man yashtari lahwa al-hadith li-yudilla 'an sabili Allah_

> "And of the people is he who buys the amusement of speech to mislead others from the way of Allah."
> — Surah Luqman, 31:6

This verse condemns amusement used to mislead people from Allah's way. It is not an anime-specific prohibition, so it should not be presented as a direct ruling on watching a series.

## Scholar Opinions

Anime did not exist in the time of classical scholars. Contemporary answers therefore apply broader principles, but they do not all give the same advice about watching videos:

- [Shaykh Irshaad Sedick, writing in a Shafi'i context](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/is-it-permissible-for-me-to-watch-anime/), permits animated television when the content is lawful. He also cautions against letting it take over one's time.
- [Shaykh Abdul-Rahim Reasat, writing in a Hanafi context](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/is-it-permissible-to-watch-anime-or-cartoons-in-islam/), says contemporary scholars differ on watching videos. If someone watches, he advises choosing wholesome content that does not promote prohibited practices.
- [Islam Question & Answer](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/225149) says watching cartoons for fun is permissible when they are free of content contrary to Islamic teaching, but recommends doing so rarely and briefly. Its answer gives a restrictive ruling on one named series; that judgment should not be applied automatically to every anime.

These are examples of published scholarly answers, not a vote count or a universal fatwa. The ruling for a particular show may depend on its content and the scholarly guidance a person follows.

## Conditions and Gray Areas

Several aspects of anime fall into gray areas that require individual judgment:

**Fictional magic and supernatural powers.** Do not assume that every invented power is the same as practicing sorcery, or that every story with magic is harmless. Ask whether a series promotes occult practice or worship of other than Allah; seek qualified advice where the distinction is unclear.

**Animated violence.** Evaluate how a show depicts gore, cruelty, and killing rather than assuming that every action scene has the same ruling.

**Romantic and sexual content.** Consider whether a show promotes prohibited relationships or includes sexually explicit scenes. The fact that characters are drawn does not settle every question about what is permissible to watch; see the differing treatment of cartoon images in the scholarly answers above.

**Time consumption.** Even halal anime becomes problematic if it leads to **missing prayers, neglecting obligations, or becoming addicted**. The principle of moderation (_wasatiyyah_) applies. If anime is consuming hours of your day and pulling you away from salah or Quran, it has become harmful regardless of its content. ## Common Questions

**Q: Are anime like Naruto, Dragon Ball, or Attack on Titan haram?**

There is no reliable title-wide answer here for Naruto, Dragon Ball, or Attack on Titan: their episodes and themes need to be assessed, and scholars may apply different standards. This article has not reviewed each series scene by scene. Check the content of the version you intend to watch and ask a qualified scholar about any point that matters to your decision.

**Q: Is watching anime a waste of time in Islam?**

Rest and recreation are not automatically forbidden. In the cited opinions that permit lawful animated content, watching for relaxation can be allowed if the content is acceptable and it does not displace obligations. Other scholars may apply stricter standards to watching videos, so moderation alone does not settle every case.

For a personal ruling, ask a qualified scholar. You can also use the [Haram Checker](/haram-check) for general background.

## Summary

The published answers above support this practical approach:

- Published scholarly answers permit some lawful animated content, while views differ about watching videos in general.
- Examine what a series contains and promotes, including sexual content, religious themes, and recurring material that conflicts with Islamic values.
- Do not let entertainment cause you to miss prayers or other obligations.
- Seek qualified advice about a specific series when its content or the ruling is unclear.

Choose content with care, guard your eyes and heart, and maintain balance in how you spend your time. The sources above provide principles for deciding what to watch, rather than a ruling on every anime title.
