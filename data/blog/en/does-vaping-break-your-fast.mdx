**Yes: deliberately vaping during an Islamic fast invalidates it under [Egypt's Dar al-Ifta ruling](https://dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/20270/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%86%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%B6%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%85), which explicitly includes electronic cigarettes.** This page concerns religious fasting, not intermittent-fasting diets or preparation for medical tests.

## What counts as breaking the fast?

The ruling concerns inhaled material entering the body. It does not make a calorie count the test for whether an electronic cigarette breaks the fast.

| Situation                               | How to understand the cited guidance                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deliberately using a vape while fasting | Electronic cigarettes are explicitly included in the ruling.                                                          |
| Using a nicotine-free vape              | The ruling gives no nicotine-free exception. A “zero nicotine” label alone does not establish that the fast is valid. |
| Unavoidable smoke around you            | The answer quotes a Hanafi passage distinguishing unavoidable smoke from deliberately drawing it in.                  |

That last distinction concerns unavoidable exposure; it does not permit deliberately inhaling someone else's smoke. If you genuinely forgot you were fasting, explain that separately to a qualified teacher instead of treating this deliberate-use answer as a ruling on your case.

## If you have already vaped

[Dar al-Ifta's answer on smoking while fasting](https://www.dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/7368/the-ruling-for-smoking-while-fasting) requires a **make-up fast, or qada**. Read together with its explicit inclusion of electronic cigarettes, the guidance means deliberately vaping is not resolved simply by stopping for the remaining hours.

Stop the vaping and ask a qualified teacher what you need to do for that day and afterward. **Do not assume that every case has the same kaffarah, or expiation.** The longer ruling quotes a conditional Hanafi discussion of expiation; this page does not calculate a personal obligation from one sentence about what happened.

Before asking, note:

- Whether this was a Ramadan fast, a make-up fast or a voluntary fast.
- Whether you remembered you were fasting when you used the vape.
- Approximately when it happened and what you used.
- The school of law you follow, if applicable.

For the wider purpose and practice of the fast, see [what sawm means in Islam](/blog/what-is-sawm). An error on one day is a reason to seek the right next step, not to abandon the rest of Ramadan.

## Plan support for cravings before the next fast

A **skin nicotine patch is a separate question from vaping**. In its [2025 answer on nicotine patches](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/21761/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%82%D8%A9-%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%83%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A3%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%85), Dar al-Ifta permits their use for a smoker trying to quit during Ramadan, with a specialist doctor's prescription. Keep that condition with the ruling; do not extend it automatically to nicotine gum, sprays or other products taken through the mouth or nose.

The [NHS explains that nicotine replacement therapy can help people quit smoking](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/quit-smoking/ready-to-quit-smoking/quit-with-nicotine-replacement-therapies-nrt/) by easing cravings and withdrawal. Ask a GP, pharmacist or stop-smoking adviser to help choose suitable support. If you are trying to stop vaping rather than smoking, say so: your circumstances and treatment needs may differ. This guide does not set a dose or tell you to change prescribed treatment.

Make the practical part simple: put the device away from your usual break spot and plan what you will do when you would normally reach for it. Those are habit-planning suggestions, not a substitute for treatment or a guarantee that cravings will disappear.

Use [fasting times for your city](/fasting-times) to check the local Fajr and Maghrib schedule, and the [Ramadan calendar](/ramadan-calendar) to plan ahead. Confirm the calculation settings and local Ramadan dates with the authority you follow.
