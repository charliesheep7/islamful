![Green title card reading Can You Do Wudu With Makeup On?](/static/images/blog/can-you-do-wudu-with-makeup-on.webp)

You can do wudu with makeup on only if water still reaches your skin. Waterproof products and thick, visible layers need to come off first. The simplest way to avoid the question is to make wudu **before** you apply makeup.

The question is not whether makeup is allowed or whether it breaks wudu. It is whether the washing was complete. This guide is built mainly on answers from one Hanafi fatwa service, Darul Ifta Birmingham, and those answers do not all draw the line in the same place. Both views are set out below.

## Quick answer

| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Waterproof product                             | No, remove it first       | [Darul Ifta Birmingham](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/home/can-you-make-wudhu-when-you-have-a-cream-on-your-face-thats-water-resistant/) |
| Mascara that is not watertight                 | Yes                       | [Darul Ifta Birmingham](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/doubts-in-removing-makeup-before-commencing-the-wudhu/)                            |
| Water-resistant sunscreen, not applied thickly | Yes                       | [Darul Ifta Birmingham](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/home/can-you-make-wudhu-when-you-have-a-cream-on-your-face-thats-water-resistant/) |
| Creams, lotions and oils                       | Yes                       | [Darul Ifta Birmingham](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/?p=29711)                                                                          |

## Why it matters: water has to reach the skin

The verse on wudu commands washing the face and the hands up to the elbows, wiping the head and washing the feet ([Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6](https://quran.com/5/6)). Hadith show that even a small missed spot matters:

- Anas reported that a man left a spot the size of a nail on his foot, and the Prophet ﷺ told him: "Go back and perform ablution well" ([Sunan Abi Dawud 173](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:173)).
- Umar ibn al-Khattab reported a similar case in which the Prophet ﷺ commanded the man to repeat both his ablution and his prayer. Darussalam grades this narration sahih ([Sunan Ibn Majah 666](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:666)).
- Abu Hurayrah told people to perform ablution thoroughly, quoting the Prophet's words: "Save your heels from the Hell-fire" ([Sahih al-Bukhari 165](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:165)).

Darul Ifta Birmingham states the resulting rule directly: wudu is invalid and incomplete if something prevents water from reaching the skin of the parts that must be washed ([Wudhu With Makeup](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/?p=31836)).

## How to tell if your makeup is a barrier

Darul Ifta Birmingham gives a working test in its answer on silicone products. A barrier affects wudu if it is **thick, visible, and you can tell it stops water from reaching the skin**, with nail polish as the example. Oil and Vaseline leave a film, but water still gets through, so wudu is valid ([silicone products](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/silicone-products/)).

Check the label too. The same service separates waterproof products, which must be removed, from water-resistant creams, which it says wash off when fully wet. Even then, it warns against applying so much that it forms a thick layer ([water-resistant cream](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/home/can-you-make-wudhu-when-you-have-a-cream-on-your-face-thats-water-resistant/)). That answer is about sunscreen and cream, so treat it as a comparison for makeup, not a ruling on a specific product.

## Where the answers differ

Ordinary foundation and eye makeup are the unclear middle, and two answers from the same service lean in different directions:

- **Stricter:** all specks of makeup must be removed before wudu and ghusl, in the same way as glue, nail polish and paint. To check, wipe the area with a tissue or cotton swab. If makeup still comes off, removal is not finished. If the swab stays clean, wudu is fine ([Darul Ifta Birmingham, fatwa 06957](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/?p=29711)).
- **More lenient:** makeup usually does not prevent water from reaching the skin. A person who found a tiny speck of eye makeup after wudu was told that their wudu and prayer should be valid ([Darul Ifta Birmingham, fatwa 07322](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/?p=31836)).

A reasonable way to read them together is that the stricter answer describes what to do before wudu when you can still remove makeup, and the lenient one reassures someone who finds a trace afterwards. That reading is ours, not theirs. If you want a firm answer for a product you wear every day, ask a scholar of your school.

## What to do in practice

**Before putting makeup on:** make wudu first, then apply your makeup. Darul Ifta Birmingham confirms you can pray with it on as long as your wudu has not broken ([fatwa 06957](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/?p=29711)). Plan this around your day using Islamful's [prayer times](/prayer-times).

**When your wudu breaks while wearing makeup:**

1. Check whether the product is waterproof or sitting in a thick layer.
2. If it is, remove it fully. Use a proper cleanser or remover, not plain water.
3. If it is not, wash each part thoroughly so water really runs over the skin. A damp wipe is not washing.
4. Finish the steps as usual, following [how to perform wudu](/blog/how-to-perform-wudu).

**If you find a real barrier after praying:** the hadith above show a man told to repeat wudu after leaving a dry spot, and in one report his prayer too. So if a waterproof layer clearly stopped water from reaching part of your face, the cautious course is to redo wudu and the prayer. A tiny speck of ordinary makeup is the situation the lenient answer above treats as fine.

**If something will not come off:** paint that can only be scraped off with a knife or a similar tool is excused because of the difficulty ([specks of paint](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/would-the-wudhu-be-valid-if-some-specs-of-paint-remain/)). That answer concerns stubborn paint, so do not stretch it to makeup you could remove with a normal cleanser.

## If checking makeup has become a struggle

Some people spend hours scrubbing at tiny specks. Darul Ifta Birmingham answered a person with OCD who was hurting their eyes this way. It recommended medical advice and cotton pads with cleanser instead of cotton buds, and reminded them that mascara that is not watertight lets water through, so wudu and ghusl are valid with it ([doubts in removing makeup](https://daruliftabirmingham.co.uk/doubts-in-removing-makeup-before-commencing-the-wudhu/)). If this sounds like you, speak to a doctor, and ask a scholar how far your cleaning needs to go.
